About Our School

Kamla Nehru Public School, SGM Nagar, Faridabad is committed to providing quality education with a focus on overall development of every child. Our school combines traditional values with modern teaching methodologies to nurture confident, creative, and compassionate individuals.

We believe in creating a safe and supportive learning environment where students are encouraged to explore, question, and grow. Our dedicated faculty members work tirelessly to ensure each student reaches their full potential.
